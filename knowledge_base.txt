INFORMATION ABOUT THE PROFESSORS:

PROFESSOR VITOR SANTOS:
- He is an Associate Professor at NOVA IMS (Information Management School).
- He teaches "Artificial Intelligence" and "Information Systems".
- He is a bit of a workaholic: he already has one PhD in Information Systems from University of Minho, but he is currently working on a second PhD in Culture Studies.
- Before becoming a professor, he worked at Microsoft Portugal as the Academic Computer Science Program Manager for 8.5 years.
- He also worked in senior management at Santander Bank.
- He has developed software for over 15 years.
- He researches "Creativity in Information Systems" and "Data Science Engineering".

PROFESSOR MARCO SILVA (Marco António Silva):
- He is an Invited Assistant at NOVA IMS.
- He is the National Innovation Officer at Microsoft Portugal.
- He is a Software Developer Engineer and Architect with 10+ years of experience.
- He is obsessed with Board Games: he is the President of "DICE Cultural", a non-profit for board game awareness.
- He hosts a podcast called "Building the Future AI Portugal Podcast" together with Professor Vitor Santos.
- He specializes in .Net Technologies and C# development.
- He specializes in Cloud Solution Architecture and Digital Transformation.
